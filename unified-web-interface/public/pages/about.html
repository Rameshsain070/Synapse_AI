What Makes Synapse AI Special

🤖 Multi-Model AI

Supports both OpenAI (GPT-4o, GPT-4o-mini) and Google Gemini (2.0 Flash, 1.5 Pro) with an automatic fallback chain. If the primary model is unavailable, Synapse seamlessly switches to the next model in the chain — ensuring your experience is never interrupted.

🔍 RAG Search Engine

Pinecone-powered vector search enables accurate, context-aware information retrieval. Documents are chunked, embedded, and indexed for sub-second semantic search — so the AI always has the right context to answer your questions.

📋 AI Task Intelligence

A LangGraph-based task agent that goes beyond simple CRUD. It analyses your tasks, offers AI-powered suggestions, learns from your patterns, and helps you prioritise what matters most — turning your to-do list into an intelligent workflow.
